Our Parish Community

St. Antony's Church, Ayroor, is a thriving parish community that has been a spiritual home for Catholic families in the region for generations. The parish is part of the Major Archdiocese of Ernakulam-Angamaly, one of the oldest Christian communities in the world.

The parish is part of the Syro-Malabar Rite — one of the most ancient Eastern Catholic traditions, tracing its roots to the apostolic mission of St. Thomas in Kerala. The parish hosts various organizations including KCYM, altar servers, choir groups, and charitable societies that together form the backbone of parish life.
