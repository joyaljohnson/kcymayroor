About YOUTURN

YOUTURN is KCYM's flagship anti-narcotic campaign that mobilizes youth across Kerala to fight the growing menace of substance abuse. The initiative combines awareness programs, community vigilance, and confidential reporting to protect our youth.

Through parish-level workshops, school awareness drives, and digital campaigns, YOUTURN has reached thousands of young people with the message that a drug-free life is a life of true freedom.

If you or someone you know is affected by substance abuse, help is just a call or message away. All reports are treated with complete confidentiality.
